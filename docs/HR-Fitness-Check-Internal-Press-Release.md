## HR Fitness Check turns HR Standard Work assessment into a continuous improvement loop

**New ORBIT product combines a monthly progress pulse, Quarterly Fitness Checks, annual Fitness Check reporting, and a governed AI assistant to help FC and Rx HR teams spend less time compiling results and more time improving Standard Work**

**Site context matters. It should never replace evidence.**

ORBIT today announced HR Fitness Check, a new quality-improvement product designed to give FC and Rx HR teams an objective, repeatable, and evidence-backed view of C03-C06 HR Standard Work.

HR Fitness Check replaces today's manually compiled, site-self-graded assessment with a governed workflow that connects evidence, findings, recommendations, human decisions, completed actions, and subsequent quality movement.

Today, site HR teams gather evidence from nine different source families, interpret results, and compile their quarterly assessments in a spreadsheet. HR Transformation then aggregates the results and develops network-level insights. The process requires significant manual effort, creates opportunities for inconsistent interpretation, and asks sites to grade their own performance.

HR Fitness Check changes that experience. The product gathers approved evidence, applies deterministic scoring rules, preserves authorized human review where physical inspection or judgment is required, and gives HR teams a clear path from finding an opportunity to taking action.

### A monthly pulse with quarterly and annual Fitness Check reporting

HR Fitness Check introduces two distinct operating rhythms.

Monthly Progress Checks help site and regional HR teams understand where performance is moving, identify emerging risks, review open actions, and address evidence gaps before the next Quarterly Fitness Check. Monthly reports are operational and provisional. They show progress, current context, and risks to the next quarterly review, but they do not certify quarterly performance.

A Quarterly Fitness Check provides the formal C03-C06 Standard Work result. Its result reaches certified state only after a frozen catalog, approved scoring rules, defined evidence windows, required manual reviews, reconciliation, and authorized sign-off. Certified Quarterly Fitness Check results become the official record for quarter-over-quarter measurement and outcome evaluation.

The annual Fitness Check report summarizes the year's certified Quarterly Fitness Checks for site, regional, FC, Rx, and network reporting. It does not create a fifth scoring run. A year-to-date version clearly identifies any quarter that has not reached certified state.

Together, this monthly pulse and quarterly/annual reporting rhythm moves HR Fitness Check beyond a point-in-time score. Teams can see where they stand each month, make adjustments while there is still time to act, and enter the Quarterly Fitness Check with fewer surprises.

### Trusted measurement comes first

Approved Standard Work measures are evaluated using deterministic rules and trusted source data. AI does not calculate or change red, yellow, or green ratings.

Each result shows the applicable evidence, measurement period, data freshness, scoring-rule version, and any relevant caveats. Missing, stale, disputed, blocked, or manual-required evidence is shown explicitly and is never silently converted into a negative rating.

Measures that require physical inspection or authorized judgment remain with HR reviewers. The product records the reviewer, evidence reference, timestamp, and correction history so manual results remain as traceable as automated results.

### A conversational agent that understands context

HR Fitness Check includes a governed conversational agent that helps HR teams interpret results, answer questions, provide relevant context, and turn opportunities into practical actions.

The agent can ask focused follow-up questions when context would improve a report or recommendation. It can also capture feedback from site and regional HR teams.

For example, if an HR leader reports that a site was short staffed during August, the agent can ask which team and dates were affected, confirm how the information may be used, and include it as attributed context in the August progress report. That context may influence the timing or feasibility of a recommendation, but it does not change the measured result.

If a user reports that the site stopped using an old SharePoint tracker and moved to Smartsheet, the agent can identify the affected Standard Work measure and create a source-change proposal. The approved source mapping does not change until the appropriate source owner validates the replacement, reconciles the data, and approves a new source version.

Users can choose whether context should be used only for the current response, saved for the current month, submitted as an ongoing site fact, or routed as a permanent source or process change. Saved context remains attributed, scoped, reviewable, and subject to expiration or withdrawal.

This creates a better user experience while protecting the integrity of the Fitness Check result.

### From insight to action—and from action to outcome

After results are calculated, AI converts the grounded evidence into site-specific strengths, opportunities, and recommended paths to green.

Regional HR reviewers evaluate each recommendation and select one of four dispositions:

- Accept.
- Modify.
- Decline.
- Defer.

The product records the reviewer, decision, rationale, and timestamp while preserving the original recommendation.

When an accepted or modified recommendation becomes an action, HR Fitness Check previews the exact action, owner, target date, and SharePoint destination. The agent records the action only after an authorized user confirms the complete payload.

At the next approved comparable measurement, the product connects the completed action to subsequent quality movement. It reports whether quality improved, remained unchanged, declined, or could not be compared. Additional comparable periods determine whether an improvement was sustained.

HR Fitness Check does not claim that an action caused a later result simply because the result followed the action. It provides transparent evidence of the sequence, execution, and observed movement so HR leaders can make informed decisions.

### What HR Fitness Check delivers

For site HR teams, the product provides:

- A monthly view of Standard Work progress.
- A formal Quarterly Fitness Check with transparent evidence and an explicit certification state.
- A clear distinction between performance issues and evidence issues.
- A work queue for missing, disputed, or manual-required items.
- Site-specific strengths, opportunities, and practical paths to green.
- A conversational way to ask questions, provide context, and challenge incorrect evidence.
- Visibility into accepted actions, owners, deadlines, and blockers.

For Regional HR, the product provides:

- Regional and site-level progress views.
- A governed recommendation-review workflow.
- Evidence and context needed to accept, modify, decline, or defer recommendations.
- Consistent rationale and decision capture.
- Action oversight and follow-up measurement.
- Visibility into recurring barriers and systemic opportunities.

For HR Transformation and network leadership, the product provides:

- Objective FC, Rx, site, region, and network quality measurement.
- Earlier visibility into emerging Standard Work risks.
- Evidence-coverage, data-quality, and source-readiness reporting.
- Network patterns that can inform process, policy, and resource decisions.
- A complete funnel from recommendation through execution and subsequent quality movement.
- Governed feedback that can improve future reports, source mappings, intervention guidance, and recommendation quality.
- A stronger record of where HR capacity is being used and what actions are producing meaningful results.

### Creating capacity for improvement

By reducing manual evidence gathering, spreadsheet compilation, and network-level assessment preparation, HR Fitness Check is estimated to make approximately 540 HR hours available annually for action rather than assessment compilation. At the current planning valuation, that represents an estimated $33,123 in annual capacity value.

These figures represent estimated capacity value, not booked or realized savings. The value will be validated through a timed current-state baseline, an approved production catalog, live-source pilot evidence, adoption measurement, and an agreed benefit-realization methodology.

The larger opportunity is what HR teams can do with that capacity: address Standard Work gaps earlier, follow through on accepted actions, support sites more consistently, and focus attention on improvements that can be verified over time.

### A governed learning loop

HR Fitness Check learns through evidence and feedback, but it does not change itself automatically.

User feedback is classified and routed according to its purpose. An evidence dispute goes to source validation. A source-change report goes to the source owner. Operational context may inform the applicable monthly narrative. Recommendation feedback helps evaluate whether the proposed action was relevant, feasible, and useful.

Feedback and outcome records are reviewed through a governed product-improvement process. Changes to prompts, source mappings, scoring rules, approved intervention guidance, or model configuration require evaluation, approval, versioning, and monitored release.

This ensures that HR Fitness Check improves with use while preserving human accountability and preventing unverified feedback from becoming production truth.

### Measuring success

HR Fitness Check will not be measured by the number of reports or AI-generated recommendations it produces.

Success will be measured by whether the product:

- Produces accurate and reproducible Standard Work results.
- Reduces assessment compilation time.
- Helps HR teams identify and address gaps earlier.
- Captures recommendation decisions consistently.
- Converts accepted recommendations into owned and completed actions.
- Resolves evidence disputes and source changes efficiently.
- Uses site context accurately without changing deterministic scores.
- Produces recommendations that HR reviewers consider relevant and actionable.
- Connects completed actions to comparable subsequent measurements.
- Shows verified and sustained improvement in HR Standard Work quality.
- Makes measurable HR capacity available for action rather than assessment compilation.
